1. Abstract

In rural and semi-urban regions across India, particularly in states like Tamil Nadu, millions of underprivileged students depend exclusively on state-run transport undertakings (such as the Tamil Nadu State Transport Corporation - TNSTC) to reach government secondary and higher secondary schools. Under Government Order G.O. Ms. No. 42, students in uniform or carrying bus passes travel free of cost. However, severe infrastructural bottlenecks persist: school feeder buses operate without live telematics, scheduled stops are often bypassed due to highway congestion, and parents at home have zero real-time visibility into whether their children boarded the bus or reached the school campus safely.

Crucially, government school regulations strictly forbid smartphones, and the overwhelming majority of students from socio-economically disadvantaged families do not own personal mobile devices. Existing private school transit systems rely heavily on expensive student tracking apps or proprietary RFID kiosks, which are entirely non-viable for public transport infrastructure.

To solve this critical societal challenge, we engineered the Smart Digital Transit & Attendance Safety Monitoring Platform. The system operates on a novel Zero-Phone Architecture: students carry durable, printed, laminated physical passes embedded with tamper-resistant QR tokens. Bus conductors and drivers utilize an ultra-low-latency in-browser scanning loop (jsQR) on ordinary mobile devices to log boarding in milliseconds. Boarding verification immediately triggers real-time cellular SMS notifications to parents' keypad or feature phones via the SMSMobileAPI telecom gateway.

Furthermore, the system integrates a MoRTH AIS-140 compliant On-Bus Physical Panic Button telemetry simulation, empowering students without phones to report rash driving, route deviations, or harassment through wall-mounted physical buttons that alert the TNSTC Depot Command Center and parents simultaneously. Upon reaching the school gate, the platform executes an automated classification algorithm that sends safe arrival confirmations to parents of boarded students while dispatching urgent "Bus Missed / Unboarded" alerts to parents whose children were absent at the stop.

2. Motivation Behind the Project

The core motivation behind this engineering project stems from real-world field reports in rural corridors (such as Dharmapuri, Krishnagiri, and Villupuram districts), where students frequently encounter skipped bus stops, long halts at railway crossings, and severe overcrowding leading to footboard accidents.

Working-class parents often work in agricultural fields or daily-wage labor and cannot accompany their children to the bus stop every morning. A delayed bus or an unverified boarding leads to immense parental anxiety and school absenteeism. By engineering an automated bridge that converts driver actions into direct cellular SMS messages—without requiring any smartphone, data pack, or app installation from the child—we provide dignity, safety, and operational transparency to public education transit.

3. Project Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to develop an inclusive, zero-cost-barrier transit safety and attendance management framework tailored specifically for government schools and state transport corporations:

  • Objective 1 (Zero-Phone Student Commute): Design and validate a laminated physical digital bus pass system utilizing encrypted 2D QR matrix codes, completely eliminating the need for students to carry smartphones.
  • Objective 2 (Continuous Camera Stream Telemetry): Implement a resilient, non-blocking webcam QR scanning engine (jsQR) with frame throttling and instant re-scan capabilities on the driver's console to mark attendance at highway speeds.
  • Objective 3 (Direct Cellular DLT SMS Dispatch): Establish cloud-to-telecom gateway dispatch through SMSMobileAPI and Fast2SMS fallback, sending transactional SMS to parent mobile numbers without requiring mobile internet on the parent's phone.
  • Objective 4 (MoRTH AIS-140 Physical Panic Alert): Simulate and integrate physical emergency SOS buttons on bus pillars, enabling in-transit students to trigger instant alarms to the depot and parent mobile phones.
  • Objective 5 (Automated Cohort Segregation upon School Arrival): Formulate an automated classification algorithm that verifies boarded commuters upon trip completion, sending safe arrival drop-off notifications to boarded students and immediate "Bus Missed" alerts to unboarded students.
  • Objective 6 (Statewide Administrative Oversight): Build a centralized live radar map using Leaflet GIS and an Admin Grievance Redressal console to monitor bus speeds, route progress, and commuter counts in real time.

4. Introduction & Societal Problem Statement

Public bus transport forms the lifeblood of rural education in India. Under state welfare schemes, students are entitled to 100% free bus travel. While this policy dramatically boosted school enrollment rates across rural Tamil Nadu, operating conditions present severe systemic challenges:

The Real-World Bottleneck: 1. Overcrowding & Skipped Stops: Overloaded buses frequently bypass intermediate village halts, leaving young students stranded.
2. Zero Transit Visibility: Parents have no tracking mechanism to ascertain whether their child boarded the morning bus.
3. The Digital Divide Paradox: High-end private school apps require parents and students to possess 5G smartphones and data subscriptions. A rural government school solution must function flawlessly with zero student mobile phones and basic 2G SMS connectivity.

8. On-Bus Physical Panic Button Hardware (AIS-140)

The Automotive Industry Standard AIS-140 (mandated by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways - MoRTH) specifies that all public passenger transport vehicles must be fitted with emergency SOS panic devices linked to vehicle tracking units (VTUs).

8.1 The Zero-Phone Dilemma Solved

If students do not have mobile phones while traveling, they cannot report driver rash driving, route deviations, harassment, or vehicle stalls. Our project bridges this gap by directly interfacing the bus's physical telemetry unit into the software ecosystem:

  • Physical Mounting Location: Physical red tactile push buttons are mounted on interior stanchion pillars adjacent to Seat #4 and the rear emergency door within easy reach of seated and standing students.
  • Autonomous Emergency Telemetry (Police & Depot Dispatch): Because the wall button is an anonymous vehicle hardware unit, it does not identify individual passengers. Instead, it triggers the onboard GSM/GPS modem to transmit emergency coordinates directly to Police Control Room (Dial 112 / ERSS) and the TNSTC Feeder Depot Command Center. (Individual parent SMS alerts are reserved for personal QR pass boarding and arrival events).
  • Driver Cabin Alarm & Safe Roadside Halt: Simultaneously, the driver dashboard receives an instant flashing red emergency siren with an audible warning chime, mandating an immediate safe vehicle pull-over to the road shoulder to attend to the passengers.

8.2 SMS Gateway Architecture on Vercel

Standard client-side browser requests to third-party SMS APIs (like SMSMobileAPI and Fast2SMS) are blocked by modern browsers due to CORS (Cross-Origin Resource Sharing). Furthermore, standard single-page application (SPA) rewrites on Vercel mistakenly rewrite API endpoints to index.html.

To solve this, we developed native Vercel Serverless Functions (/api/smsmobile.js and /api/fast2sms.js) configured with negative lookahead routing in vercel.json:

// vercel.json (Negative Lookahead Regex Routing)
{
  "rewrites": [
    { "source": "/((?!api/).*)", "destination": "/index.html" }
  ]
}

9.2 Resilient jsQR Video Loop Engine

In early iterations, scanning an invalid or unrecognized QR code (such as a Wikipedia URL) caused the animation loop to terminate prematurely. We refactored the camera subsystem with React useRef locks, eliminating closure staleness:

  • requestAnimationFrame(scanVideoLoop) is scheduled unconditionally on every frame, guaranteeing the camera stream never locks up.
  • Identical unrecognized frames are debounced with a 2-second cooldown to eliminate frame jitter, while valid student passes trigger instant verification.

10. Automated Boarded vs Missed Classification

A common flaw in conventional school transport tracking is broadcasting generic "Bus Reached School" messages to all enrolled route parents. If a student woke up late and never boarded the bus, their parents would receive a misleading message stating their child reached campus.

10.1 The Segregated Dual-SMS Pipeline

When the bus driver terminates the morning route at the school gate (via the "Mark Bus Arrived at School Gate" action), our cloud engine partitions route students into two mutually exclusive cohorts:

Commuter Cohort Boarding Flag Synchronized Status Dispatched Cellular SMS Template
Boarded Commuters isBoarded: true
isMissed: false		 Safely Arrived at School Gate "TN-GOVT-EDU OFFICIAL: Bus 14A safely reached Government School Gate at 08:30 AM. Your ward Prem (10th Std) has safely entered campus. Free Govt Bus."
Unboarded Commuters isBoarded: false
isMissed: true		 Bus Missed / Not Boarded "TN-GOVT-EDU ALERT: Bus 14A completed route and reached School Gate at 08:30 AM. Your ward Student 1 (10th Std) was NOT boarded at Morappur Bus Stand (BUS MISSED). Please verify with ward."

10.2 State Synchronization Across All Portals

Upon arrival execution, the central database broadcasts updates to all active sessions:

  • Driver Console: Counters immediately shift to "X Arrived • Y Missed". Unboarded students show red badges with a "Late Board" manual override option if the student was picked up along the way.
  • Parent Tracker & Student Pass: Unboarded students see a red alert: "⚠️ Bus Missed / Not Boarded: Bus completed route to School Gate." Boarded students display the green safe drop-off badge.

12. Conclusion & Future Roadmap

The Smart Digital Transit & Attendance Safety Monitoring System successfully demonstrates that enterprise-grade commuter safety, real-time tracking, and automated parent communication can be achieved for public government schooling without forcing expensive smartphones or mobile internet subscriptions upon rural students.

By harmonizing durable printed QR passes, in-browser rear-camera scanning, Vercel cloud serverless telecom dispatch, and AIS-140 physical vehicle panic telemetry, the platform provides complete peace of mind to parents, drivers, and transport administrators alike.

Future Roadmap: 1. Hardware prototype of an ESP32 microcontroller with a physical illuminated red button connected directly to vehicle CAN-bus OBD-II telemetry.
2. Automated speech broadcast in Tamil for bus stop arrival announcements inside the bus cabin.
3. Machine-learning models to predict feeder corridor overcrowding based on seasonal agricultural harvests and school exam schedules.
